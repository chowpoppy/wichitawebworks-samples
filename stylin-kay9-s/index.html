Our story

About Stylin' Kay9's

Kayla started grooming at 16 as a bather at a local grooming, daycare and boarding business, and opened her own grooming business at 19. She owned a storefront grooming shop for 9 years.

Since April 2024 she has groomed from a studio salon in her home near Palmer Park and Powers. She brings 18 years of experience to every dog, and she is Fear Free and Canine CPR certified.
