Our story

Churned fresh, right here

The Sweet Tee is a family-owned, neighborhood ice cream counter. Every batch is hand-churned in the shop with Hiland Dairy milk and served in waffle cones made in-house.

It shares a building with 8 Iron Therapy indoor virtual golf, so it's an easy add-on to a party or a round.
